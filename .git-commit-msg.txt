feat(web): the conversation list learns to rename and delete, wiring the route the endpoint gate had written off

`/conversations/{id}` has answered PATCH and DELETE since the service shipped, while the
frontend's own endpoint gate carried the reason "chats are created and read, never renamed
or deleted from the app". The sidebar now does both: a hover pencil turns the row into an
inline editor (Enter saves, Escape cancels, and a blank or unchanged draft never goes out --
the backend would silently ignore it), and a trash icon opens the destructive confirm, after
which the transcript cache of the deleted chat is dropped rather than invalidated. Deleting
the chat you are looking at deselects it first, so a dead transcript never stays on screen.

Both gates follow: the endpoint gate's unwired list shrinks by its own rule (it may only
shrink), the toast census moves 28 -> 30, and the invalidation walk records 45 promises, all
still naming a declared key a screen mounts.
